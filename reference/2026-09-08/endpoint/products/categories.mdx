---
title: "List categories"
openapi: "/reference/2026-09-08/openapi.json GET /product/categories"
description: "Get a list of all possible product categories"
---

Returns a list of all possible product categories, these correspond to the `categories` field in the [list products](/reference/2026-09-08/endpoint/products/list) and [get product details](/reference/2026-09-08/endpoint/products/get) endpoints.
