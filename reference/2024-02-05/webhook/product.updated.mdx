An event is triggered when there is an update to a product in the catalog. If multiple products are updated at once, an event is triggered for each product.

The event uses the [Standard Webhooks](https://www.standardwebhooks.com/) envelope. The event type is in `type`, always `product.updated` for this event, so you can dispatch on it if your endpoint receives more than one event type. The product change is in `data`, and `timestamp` indicates when the update occurred.

Use `data.product_code` to identify the product that was updated, then refer to `data.old_state` and `data.new_state` to determine the changes.

The `price_multiplier` is the multiplier applied to the price of an order item, equal to 1 minus your discount. For example, a 10% discount gives a `price_multiplier` of `0.9`.

<Info>
  Due to the nature of webhooks you should not rely on the order of the events.
  You should store the envelope `timestamp` and discard any events that are
  older than the last event you received for the product.
</Info>
